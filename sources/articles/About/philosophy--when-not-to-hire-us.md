# /philosophy/when-not-to-hire-us

---

- The source doc lists explicit refusals — things Creative Navy won't do. These are the clearest signal of who this is not for.

REFUSALS FROM SOURCE DOC:
- Won't perform research as ritual (collecting data to look legitimate)
- Won't validate designs (can only observe behaviour and refine)
- Won't apply patterns without understanding (cargo cult design)
- Won't persuade stakeholders (explains until they see it themselves — different dynamic)
- Won't rush past ambiguity (the unclear space is where solutions form)
- Won't accept client frames uncritically (product thinking often needs refinement)
- Won't deliver hollow interfaces (better to have less but with substance)
- Won't optimise for launch day over operational longevity
- Won't fragment coherence to accommodate every local preference

DISQUALIFYING SITUATIONS TO DERIVE FROM THESE:

- Projects that need fast surface delivery: the method takes longer than clients who expect linear progress are used to. Sandbox Experiments alone can take 2–3 months for complex platforms. If speed to market is the primary constraint, this is not the right fit.

- Organisations not ready to transfer understanding: Phase 4 requires organisational participation. If the client doesn't want to be involved in the thinking — wants a deliverable handed over — the method doesn't transfer what it's designed to transfer.

- Problems that are primarily strategy problems, not systems design problems: if the organisation hasn't yet decided what to build or who to serve, UX design work is premature. The method operates on a problem that has at least enough definition to design within.

- Organisations that want validation rather than evidence: if the decision is already made and the brief is to confirm it, that's not what this is for.

- Organisations that need to accommodate every stakeholder preference: the method doesn't fragment coherence to keep everyone comfortable. If internal politics require that every competing priority is honored in the design, the result won't be coherent.

- Note: this page is structurally important for the site because it signals confidence and specificity. Agencies that claim to work with everyone signal that they don't have a method. Explicit disqualifiers build credibility with the right clients.

- Tone: honest and direct, not apologetic. These are genuine situations where the engagement would not produce value, not false modesty.
